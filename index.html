Welcome to Chestoku 2.5

This version uses a standard 8×8 chessboard and English interface. Sudoku uses digits 1–8 and 2×4 regions. Stage 1: you play White. Stage 2: you play Black. Stage 3: your side is chosen randomly.

Standard chess castling is supported. Move a piece; if it lands on an empty Sudoku square, enter the correct digit. Stage 1 needs 6 points, Stage 2 needs 10, and Stage 3 needs 14. Checkmate wins any stage.
