Welcome to Chestoku!

A strategy blend of Classic Sudoku & Chess Rules

1. Standard Sudoku

Fill digits 1 through 9. Each digit must appear exactly once in every row, column, and 3x3 subgrid.

2. Chess Special Constraints

  • King (K):

    Surrounding 8 adjacent cells cannot contain consecutive digits to the King cell.

  • Knight (N):

    Cells a Knight's L-move away cannot share the same or consecutive numbers.

  • Bishop (B):

    All cells along the Bishop's four diagonal rays must have the same even/odd parity as the Bishop.

Tapping on any cell with a Chess badge highlights all affected attack spaces on the grid!
